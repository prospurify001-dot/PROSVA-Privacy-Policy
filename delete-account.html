PROSVA Account Deletion Request

PROSVA respects your privacy and provides users with a way to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Important: Account deletion requests are reviewed to verify the request and protect users from unauthorized deletion requests.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your PROSVA account and associated personal data, submit a request through the contact method provided in the PROSVA Google Play Store listing.

In your request, please provide enough information for PROSVA to identify your account, such as the email address or phone number associated with your PROSVA account.

What Will Be Deleted

Depending on the information associated with your account, deletion may include:

  • Your PROSVA account profile information
  • Account-associated contact information
  • Saved account preferences
  • Other personal information associated with your account

Information That May Need to Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law, necessary for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or dispute-resolution purposes. Where information must be retained, it will be kept only for the period required for the applicable purpose.

Deletion Processing

After receiving and verifying a valid deletion request, PROSVA will process the request and delete eligible personal data associated with the account, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

Need Help?

If you have questions about account deletion or your personal data, please use the contact information provided in the official PROSVA Google Play Store listing.

You can also review the PROSVA Privacy Policy for additional information about how PROSVA handles user information.
